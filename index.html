Privacy Policy: Hain!

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Developer: Symflo IT Solutions

Contact: support@symflo.dev

In short: Hain! collects almost nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It talks only to your app store and, for optional purchases, to RevenueCat, the service that keeps a record of what was bought. Nothing about you or how you play goes to us.

What the game keeps on your device

To let you continue where you left off, Hain! saves on your device only:

  • your progress: the days played, stars, coins, and the upgrades and decorations bought;
  • how you arranged and decorated each venue;
  • what you bought in the game with real money, so it is there the next time you play;
  • your settings: language, sound, reduced motion, colorblind palette and hints.

This stays in the app's own storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Purchases (optional)

The game sells a few optional extras for real money: looks for your eatery (they change nothing in the game) and tips for the cook. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card, account or payment details.

To know what you've bought and give it back on a new phone or after reinstalling, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • your purchases: what you bought, when, and the store's receipt for it;
  • an app user ID: a random one, made by the game on your device, not linked to your name, email or store account;
  • basic device information, such as the device model, operating system, app version and country (for prices in your currency).

We use these only to unlock and restore what you bought and to count what was sold. They are not used for advertising or tracking. If you never buy anything, RevenueCat has no purchases from you, only the ID and device information above.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Like every developer, we get the stores' sales reports (what was sold, in which country, for how much), which do not tell us who you are.

What we collect

Only the purchase record above, kept by RevenueCat for us. We do not collect, store, share or sell any other personal or usage data, and the game asks you for no device permissions.

Third parties

The game's one third-party service is RevenueCat, for purchases. There is no advertising, analytics or sign-in. Apple's App Store and Google Play, where you download the game and make any purchase, handle their own data under their own privacy policies.

Children

Hain! is suitable for all ages. It asks no one for personal information: a purchase is recorded the same way for everyone, under a random ID, never a name or contact detail.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.

Contact
